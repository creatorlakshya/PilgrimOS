About the Sacred Abode

Standing majestically on the western banks of the holy river Ganga, the Kashi Vishwanath Temple is the spiritual heart of Varanasi. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, known here as Vishvanatha or Vishveshvara—meaning "Ruler of the Universe"—this ancient shrine houses one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas. For millennia, pilgrims have flocked to Kashi in search of liberation (Moksha), drawn by the belief that a single glimpse of the golden spire cleanses the soul of lifetimes of karma.

The current temple structure was built in 1780 by the noble Maratha queen, Ahilyabai Holkar of Indore, while its famous domes were gilded with over 800 kilograms of pure gold donated by Maharaja Ranjit Singh in 1835. The newly constructed Kashi Vishwanath Corridor now seamlessly connects the historic temple with the ancient river ghats, combining centuries-old architectural heritage with world-class civic infrastructure for a dignified yatra.
