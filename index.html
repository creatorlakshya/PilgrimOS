Why PilgrimOS

Everything your yatra deserves

From first darshan to final aarti, every moment of your sacred journey — managed with the precision it deserves.

01

Darshan Slot Booking

"No more queues from 4 AM" — Reserve your darshan window days in advance. Walk in at your time, skip the crowds, worship in peace.

  • Choose preferred darshan time & duration
  • QR-coded digital passes, no printing needed
  • Real-time waitlist and slot availability
  • Instant confirmation via SMS & app
02

Aarti & Puja Booking

"Sacred rituals, confirmed in seconds" — Select your puja type, choose a pandit, add gotram details, and receive a digital yajamana receipt.

  • 100+ puja types across all traditions
  • Select pandit by specialization & language
  • Live aarti seating with tier selection
  • Prasad delivery to your doorstep
03

Live Crowd Intelligence

"Know before you go" — Real-time crowd levels, wait time predictions, and the best windows to visit — powered by sensor data and ML.

  • Live heatmaps of temple premises
  • Hourly crowd forecast for next 7 days
  • Parking and transport availability
  • Alert when crowd drops below threshold
04

Donation Tracking

"Give with trust, see the impact" — Every rupee donated is tracked on a public ledger. See exactly where your offering goes and what it built.

  • Dedicated temple donation accounts
  • 80G tax benefit receipts instantly
  • Impact reports: meals, renovations, events
  • Recurring donations & festival pledges
